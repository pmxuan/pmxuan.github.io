I graduated from the **University of Science, VNU-HCM (HCMUS)** with a major in **Data Science**, under the supervision of Prof. [Bac Le](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=UA_83MUAAAAJ&hl=vi) and Assoc. Prof. [Tung Kieu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9luXdEIAAAAJ&hl=en).

Currently, I am pursuing a **Master’s degree** at the **Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST)** under the supervision of Prof. [Masashi Unoki](https://scholar.google.co.jp/citations?user=nXje6ywAAAAJ&hl=ja).


# 💻 Research & Professional Interests

My research interests include **Data Mining** and **Signal Processing**.

I specialize in building scalable data pipelines and intelligent chatbot systems. I enjoy working with complex datasets, designing efficient system architectures, and developing practical solutions to real-world problems through data-driven approaches.



# 🌱 Research Motivation

I am always eager to explore emerging advancements in **Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Big Data**. Whether experimenting with state-of-the-art machine learning frameworks or implementing cutting-edge cloud solutions, I thrive on innovation, continuous learning, and impactful research.
